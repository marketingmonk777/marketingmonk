MarketingMonk Brand Kit

Calm marketing. Relentless follow-up. The monk stands for patience and focus; the halo doubles as a chat bubble, because every lead starts with a conversation.

Logo

The mark animates: the monk floats, the halo breathes, and the typing dots blink. It holds still for visitors who turn on reduced motion. Keep clear space equal to the halo's width; never stretch or recolour the monk.
